What got me into spinning?

Why would I do this to myself

It started at the enumclaw shepard's festival called the [Shepard's Extravaganza](https://shepherds-extravaganza.com/). Where local shepards could showcase their best fleeces of the year, local dyers would showcase their brilliant colored wool and yarn, and local mills could showcase all the different services they offered ranging from just washing your sheep's fleece to turning raw wool into yarn to creaing compost pellets meant for your garden.

I was new on the scene, had heard about this event from a Bainbridge knitting circle at La Mercerie when it was still in it's old location near Pegasus cafe. It was overwhelming at first but so vibrant. It was like an underground community was revealing itself to the light. A lot of these vendors don't have websites or facebook pages and rely on these trade shows . A lot of these vendors do it for the love of the craft and most, if not all, vendors don't rely on this work as their main income stream. Blue Mist Farms for example has a fiber mill, a working farm, and an Aibnb business, and those are the only protions of their business I know about.
